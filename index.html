Yuelyu Ji

Ph.D. Student, Information Science · School of Computing and Information

I build language systems that retrieve, reason, and can be checked. Long-form answers from retrieval-augmented models mix supported, under-evidenced, and contradicted statements, and a single score for the whole answer hides the one sentence that matters. My work breaks generations down to atomic claims and verifies each against the evidence — then turns those signals into training rewards for search agents that know when to look something up.

I am advised by Dr. Daqing He in the iRiS Lab, and work with Pitt’s Department of Biomedical Informatics on biomedical NLP. I have been an Applied Science Intern at Microsoft (Word Copilot agent evaluation) and Amazon (multimodal RAG benchmarking).

RAG-RLRC-LaySum

Retrieval-augmented generation with reinforcement-learned readability control for lay summarization of biomedical text. BioLaySumm @ ACL 2024.

MedRAGChecker

Decomposes biomedical RAG answers into atomic claims and verifies each one, fusing textual NLI with a knowledge-graph signal through a selective gating rule. Code for the EMNLP 2026 paper.

medchecker-verl

Reinforcement-learning training loop (VeRL) for medical answer verification, coupling a checker reward with a generator policy.
